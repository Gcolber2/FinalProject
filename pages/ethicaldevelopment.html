Ethical Issues in Web Development

What are the Ethics of Web Development

Web Development ethics pertain to the proper use of material created by other persons or businesses on or in your webpage. This can range from the code that makes up a webpage, the programs used therein, or the media and links it contains. For all of these things that have a source from someone other than the owner of the site it is best to make sure what the status of copyright/copyleft for these items may be. However whether they are fair use or require a legal process before use, it is always vitally important to credit those whose creations you make use of both to honor any agreements between the rights holder and you or to avoid coming afoul of a rule/law pertaining to usage rights fair or otherwise. It is also a matter of convenience for introducing new viewers to someone's work and will reflect well on you in the long run.
