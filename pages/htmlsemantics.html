HTML - Semantic Elements

What are the Semantic Elements of HTML?

The semantic elements of HTML are elements that have a meaning clear to both machine and human code readers. Their use lies in their readability, accesibility, and consistency in the code. It makes it easier for screen readers for those with disabilities to parse the information on the page and how to read what and where. It makes it easier to look through the code with your own eyes and see where errors may have occured and to better remember what exactly has been changed after the fact as there's less text to remember now.


Semantics Examples.

  • Header: Describes the heading of a particular webpage or section. Goes from h1 - h6 with h1 being the biggest and h6 the smallest.
  • Section/Article: Both are used for sectioning off content from the rest of a given site/document. Article being used more for for self contained compositions, and section being for standalone sections. Both also usually have headers.
  • Aside: Is used for content that is related to the main text in some way but isn't part of the flow of it. Like the author's info or extra links about the subject matter.
  • Footer: Used for the bottom of the document usually and is mainly used for metainformation.
