Using Generative Artificial Intelligence

What is Generative AI?

Generative Artificial Inteligence or GenAI is a subcategory of AI that serves the purpose of generating various forms of content using user prompts. This content is formed from the collective data it collects from various user inputs and various forms of media and information that it has been fed. Its ability to have prompts asked of it using natural language as of recent along with it's speed in following those prompts and its overall availability has led to a sharp increase in usage of it. Whether this be for generating art, code, video, text(ChatGPT), pictures, and etc it has many uses that people work with.

Ethics Concerns

However it is also because of it's generative nature that there are various issues and concerns that must be taken into account. These include information outputed being biased or incorrect due to a flawed knowledge base that it pulls from. Concerns of copyrighted material being plagarised by it as it reads data. It needing to be double checked as it can accidentaly make up information or details due to an error in it's knowledge base. These things and more require careful checking up on the AI and making sure to iron out errors as they appear.
